# Portfolio reading experience

Status: Implemented; verification tracked in [progress](../progress.md). See [content coverage](portfolio-content-coverage.md) for publication decisions.

**Purpose.** Separate introduction, resume scanning, and deep reading; retain every supplied project/experience fact. Serve recruiters and engineers. Reading requires no sign-in.

**Navigation.** Home, Resume & Work, Ask me, Contact. Home presents intro, philosophy, current direction, interests, and a clear work link. Resume & Work lists every role and personal project: identity, dates, short summary, and strongest achievement bullets. Section links lead to Experience, Projects, Education, Skills. Employer projects stay under their employer; personal work links to project entries.

**Detail flow.** Titles open complete pages at the top; an achievement's explicit “Read details” link opens its supporting section. Use stable anchors, a briefly highlighted target, accessible focus, and sticky-header clearance. Offer a contents list and Back to overview. Browser Back restores overview position and focus; direct arrivals receive a useful overview link. Preserve existing URLs. Prefer full pages for long reading; defer optional desktop previews. Print outputs the concise resume.

**Completeness.** Before release, reconcile the original write-up, published content, and rendered pages. Map every supplied fact to a visible destination or an explicit publication decision; keep private/unreviewed material out of public content. Summaries supplement full text. Detail pages retain context, ownership, problem, solution, impact, tradeoffs, technologies, evidence, and references where supplied. Never invent results.

**Acceptance.** Every entry is discoverable; links reach supporting text. Test long/mobile content, keyboard, reduced motion, duplicate headings, stale anchors (fall back to entry), refresh/shared links, interrupted loading, retryable failures, empty sections, and content changes during navigation. No dead links, nested scrolling, or color-only target cues. Audit coverage before declaring completeness.
